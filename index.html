What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Plan, seat and add-on prices are the published figures. Message rates come from this region's Wati rate card, effective 1 October 2026, billed on the recipient's country. Higher tiers are cheaper per message.
  2. Message rates are quoted in USD on the rate card. Where the plan is billed in another currency, the two are converted into the currency picked at the top of the quote panel, at today's rate. On this rate card service messages are charged at the utility rate.
  3. The rate cards are linked under question 1 — open the source on a call rather than quoting from memory.
  4. The estimate picks the cheapest plan that fits. Pin a plan to quote higher; anything it can't cover is left out of the total and flagged.
  5. Growth's 15,000 broadcast cap is checked against marketing plus utility. Authentication is API-triggered one-to-one and doesn't count.
  6. Included triggers: 1,000 / 2,000 / 5,000. Extras are priced in bands — you pay the first band that covers you.
  7. Included Wati AI credits: 2,000 / 5,000 / 20,000, one pool across Wati AI and Astra. Extras are banded and don't roll over.
  8. Astra agents come with the plan: 1 / 2 / unlimited, Voice AI on Business only. Not a separate subscription.
  9. Extra WhatsApp numbers need Business. Shopify is $4.99 a month in USD, so it moves with the exchange rate.
  10. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice.
  11. Volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month aren't modelled. Also excluded: dedicated onboarding, technical account management, professional services and green-tick verification.
  12. Exchange rates come from Frankfurter, falling back to Exchange Rate API. Both publish once a day, so the rate is a daily reference rate, not a dealing rate. Wati invoices in SGD; your bank's rate on the day will differ slightly.
